# Golden Romance

Audience: Uzbek-speaking wedding guests opening a personal invitation in a mobile messenger.

Inventory: Kamoliddin; Parvinaxon; K & P; the supplied 1100 × 1021 warm heart-blurred portrait; 13 November 2026; Friday; 18:00 Asia/Tashkent; Shabnam; Axmedovlar and Normurodovlar families; guest-specific greeting.

Argument: a sealed letter is addressed to you → opening it reveals the couple's names → their actual portrait carries the emotion → a personal Uzbek greeting invites you → a November calendar saves the confirmed start → the celebration and venue provide practical details → the names return as the closing signature.

Idea: Kamoliddin and Parvinaxon's two names become the signature on an unfolding letter, from the K & P seal to the closing page.

Direction: name-as-monument, seed 46521085. User constraints override dealt collages, stock photography, four-image floor, email contact, pinned scrolling and cropped text: only one authentic supplied photograph exists; the names must stay complete; this is an invitation rather than a portfolio. The requested paper shadows, floral line drawing and gold frame intentionally override the skill's generic restrictions on ornament, thin rules and shadows.

Reference principles: Greenvelope's paper stationery treatment (https://www.greenvelope.com/resources/digital-invitations-that-look-like-real-stationery); The Letterist's emphasis on the couple's names and typography (https://www.theletterist.com/journal). No third-party images, text or designs copied.

Palette: ivory #FCF6EC, pearl #FFFDF8, champagne #E8D5BC, chocolate #312B28; #B58B49 is decorative gold, darker gold for accessible text. Cormorant Garamond display and Manrope body, locally served.

Copy deck: Toʻy taklifnomasi / Taklifnomani ochish / Kamoliddin & Parvinaxon / Ikki qalb. Bir taqdir. / Bir muhabbat. Bir umr. / Aziz mehmonimiz! / Hayotimizdagi eng quvonchli kunimizni siz bilan birga nishonlashni istaymiz. Sizni nikoh toʻyimizga taklif qilamiz. / 13 noyabr 2026, Juma, 18:00 / Taqvimga qoʻshish / Baxtimizga sherik boʻling / Shabnam toʻyxonasi / Sizni toʻyimizda kutib qolamiz!

Motion: one-time paper opening, 1300ms; staggered names and one-time scroll reveals, 600ms with 70ms increments; control feedback 160–200ms. Transform and opacity only; reduced-motion opens in 120ms and removes travel. No scroll lock, snapping, particles or animation dependency for reading.
